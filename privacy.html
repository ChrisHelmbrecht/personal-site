Privacy.

What happens to your data when you visit this site. It is not much.

In short

  • This site sets no cookies, counts no visitors and uses no tracking or advertising tools.
  • There is no form. If you write to me, you do so with your own mail program.
  • The server keeps the usual log of requests. Your browser keeps a few settings for the site, on your device only.
  • One outside service can come into play: Mixcloud’s player on the DJ page, and only after you press Play.

Hosting and server logs

The site lies on a server of the cloud provider DigitalOcean, LLC (USA). The server stands in Frankfurt am Main. With every request the server processes data that is technically necessary and writes it to a log file: the IP address, date and time, the address requested, the page you come from (referrer), and browser and operating system. I use this data only to deliver the site, to find errors and to fend off attacks, and I do not combine it with other data. The legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR: my legitimate interest in running the site safely and reliably. The log files are rotated automatically: at most ten files of 10 MB each are kept, older ones are deleted.

A data processing agreement with DigitalOcean is in place. For transfers to the USA, DigitalOcean relies under that agreement on its certification under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, and otherwise on the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses. More in DigitalOcean’s data processing agreement.

What your browser keeps for the site

There are no cookies here. So that the site can remember a few things, your browser puts them into the local storage of your device: the language you chose, whether the sound is on or off, your setting for the motion in the blog, and the best scores of the games, in Owl Night Long with the three initials you enter yourself. For the length of your visit only, it also remembers which page you come back to the skull from and whether you allowed the tilting.

This information stays on your device and is not sent to me or to anyone else. You can delete it at any time in your browser’s settings. The legal basis is § 25 (2) no. 2 of the German Telecommunications Digital Services Data Protection Act (TDDDG): the storage is necessary for functions you call up yourself.

The motion sensor

On a phone the skull can follow the way you hold the device. For that the site reads the motion sensor, on an iPhone only after you have allowed it. The values are used in your browser for the picture alone. They are not stored and not sent anywhere.

Mixcloud

The recordings on the DJ page play through the player of Mixcloud (Mixcloud Limited, Unit #2255, 275 New North Road, London N1 7AA, United Kingdom). The player is loaded when you press Play, not before. Until then no request goes to Mixcloud. From then on Mixcloud receives your IP address and technical details of your browser, and it can set cookies and process further data. If you are signed in to Mixcloud, it can connect the listening to your account. I have no influence on that.

The legal basis is your consent, given by pressing Play (Art. 6 (1) (a) GDPR, § 25 (1) TDDDG). You can withdraw it for the future by not starting the player again; cookies already set can be deleted in your browser. For the United Kingdom there is an adequacy decision of the European Commission. More in Mixcloud’s privacy policy.

Links to other sites

The site links to other services, for example Instagram, SoundCloud, Mixcloud, Facebook, Telegram, YouTube, the shop at Spreadshop, fuckingmoskau.de and dieneue.social. These are plain links. Only when you click one does that provider learn of your visit, and then its own privacy rules apply.

Fonts, pictures, films and scripts come from this site’s own server. Nothing is loaded from other companies.

When you write or call

If you send me an e-mail or call me, I process what you tell me (for example your name, your address and the content of your message) in order to answer. The legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR where it is about a contract or the steps towards one, a booking for instance, and otherwise Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR. I delete messages when they are no longer needed and no legal retention period stands against it. The mailbox is kept at IONOS SE in Germany.

I object to the use of the contact details in the legal notice for advertising that was not asked for.

Encryption

The site is transmitted in encrypted form (TLS). You can tell by the “https” in the address.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right of access (Art. 15), to rectification (Art. 16), to erasure (Art. 17), to restriction of processing (Art. 18) and to data portability (Art. 20). Consent you have given can be withdrawn at any time for the future.

Right to object (Art. 21 GDPR): where I process data on the basis of Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR, you can object at any time on grounds arising from your particular situation. I then no longer process the data, unless there are compelling legitimate grounds that outweigh your interests, or the processing serves to establish, exercise or defend legal claims.

For all of this an e-mail to chris@chrishelmbrecht.com is enough.

You can also complain to a data protection supervisory authority. The one responsible for me is the Bavarian State Office for Data Protection Supervision (Bayerisches Landesamt für Datenschutzaufsicht), Promenade 18, 91522 Ansbach, Germany.
