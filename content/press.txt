# The press page: one block per piece. scripts/build-press.py makes press.html and de/press.html from this.
#
#   clip:      a short name for the piece, never shown (cnn-2009)
#   section:   tv | radio | print        (print is also "online")
#   medium:    who published or broadcast it
#   programme: the programme or series, if there is one (Punkt 12, Neugier genügt)
#   title:     the piece's own title, in its own language, exactly as published. Leave empty rather than make one up
#   date:      2009-05-05, 2014-04 or 2020. Leave empty if not known
#   lang:      de | en | ru   (the language of the piece)
#   url:       where it can still be read, heard or seen. Empty: the piece is listed as "Archive"
#   note_en / note_de: one plain line on what it is. Facts only
#   source:    how the facts above are known. Never shown
#   draft:     yes keeps the piece off the pages until it is checked
#
# Only what is documented goes in. A title, a date or a broadcaster that is not certain stays empty, or the piece stays a draft.
