Chris Helmbrecht

DJ, author of the book Fucking Moskau and brand consultant in Munich. This site is one drawing: a skull in blue ink holding an owl, the moon and the skylines of Moscow, Munich, Tenerife and New York, the cities of his life. Every part of the skull is a door: the moon leads to DJ, the owl to the bio, the trees to the blog, the Twin Towers to press, the path to the timeline and the teeth to contact. A making-of hides on the right cheekbone and an arcade game on the left.
