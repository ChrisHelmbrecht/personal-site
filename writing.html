A writer at heart.

For more than 30 years, I’ve been sharing stories through writing. My early days were filled with interviews featuring iconic bands like the Beastie Boys, Metallica, Suicidal Tendencies, and many others. I also chronicled my adventures as a professional snowboarder in Germany’s top snowboarding magazines.

I’m a keen observer, diving headfirst into risky, exhilarating adventures and reaping fascinating stories as rewards. These true experiences fuel the content of my blogs and have even found their way into my published work.

As a contributor to Stern.de and the magazine Stern, I’ve brought readers closer to my unique perspective. These days, I continue writing for clients, crafting compelling blog posts, and creating concepts for TV documentaries. Recently, my storytelling has extended into filmmaking, where I’ve been involved in various productions.

Author of “Fucking Moskau!”

In 2013, my book “Fucking Moskau!” was published in Germany by Heyne Verlag/Randomhouse. It’s a bold, no-holds-barred journey into the world of unbridled luxury, unrestrained indulgence, and the breathtaking contrasts of Moscow – a city of both frozen extremes and fiery encounters.

Moscow is a city that dazzles with its contradictions. I’ve encountered staggering wealth and a relentless hunger for life and pleasure. In “Fucking Moskau!” I share stories of vodka-soaked nights, outrageous oligarch parties, and the insatiable thirst for money and hedonism that defines this vibrant metropolis.
