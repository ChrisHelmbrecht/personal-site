The moon

Storytelling with music.

As a native of Munich, DJ, music enthusiast, and cultural manager, I have journeyed through the music world over the past three decades, deepening my passion for DJing and the electronic music scene.

With over 30 years of DJ experience, I am no stranger to the scene. Originally hailing from Munich, I left the city more than 25 years ago to live in New York, Spain, and Moscow. During this time, I not only explored a diverse range of music genres but also performed on stages worldwide. My musical journey has taken me from the streets of New York to the clubs of Milan, from a techno party in Tehran to the vibrant nights in Tenerife, and many other places.

My DJ career began in my school days when I became known for my mixtapes and playing at house parties. Music became my lifeblood, and I developed a special taste for hip-hop, RnB, and later electronic music. During my time as an extreme snowboarder in the 90s, I organized some very successful (and well-known) snowboarder parties in the Alps.

The diversity of my musical journey is also reflected in my performances. From Formula 1 parties and 5-star hotels to legendary techno clubs in Moscow and various underground scenes – I have explored a wide range of styles.

Today, I am not just a DJ but also a curator of musical experiences. My sets are a blend of melodic techno, Afro, and indie dance.
